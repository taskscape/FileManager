Reorganize

Preview a reorganization in the reorg: panel, then apply it. Files are moved by the host. Nothing is copied or permanently deleted.

Before applying, open Plan Review, check the listed steps, and choose Mark as Reviewed. Apply runs only the steps that were reviewed; after any change to the plan or to the folders, review it again.

Folders that the plan leaves empty are moved to the recovery store, not deleted. Turn this off or on with Clean up emptied folders in Plan Review; the step list updates at once, and the new list must be marked as reviewed before applying.
